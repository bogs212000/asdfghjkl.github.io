Privacy Policy

This policy explains how eMarket PH collects, uses, protects, and retains information when people use the eMarket mobile marketplace app.

Effective date: July 16, 2026 Company: eMarket PH Built by: Garry Earl Lontes

Privacy Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion support, contact us by email.

Email

emarketph.sup@gmail.com
01

Information We Collect

We collect account information including username, email address, phone number, password, birthday or age information, profile photo, and location details. Seller verification may include valid ID images and a camera selfie.

We also collect listings, listing photos, reports, favorites, messages, device notification tokens, app activity, analytics, crash logs, and approximate or precise location when needed for nearby marketplace features.

02

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the eMarket marketplace.
  • Display nearby listings and seller profiles.
  • Review ID and selfie verification submissions.
  • Send notifications about messages, reports, and account activity.
  • Show Google AdMob banner or rewarded ads.
  • Detect, prevent, and review fraud, scams, unsafe behavior, and policy violations.
03

Payments and Transactions

eMarket PH does not process, handle, or store payment information for buyer-seller transactions. Payment method, meet-up arrangement, delivery, and transaction completion are arranged directly between buyers and sellers outside the app.

04

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information. Data may be shared with other users when needed for marketplace interaction, with trusted service providers that help operate the app, or when required by law or government authorities.

05

Firebase, AdMob, and Service Providers

eMarket PH uses Firebase and Google services for authentication, database, storage, messaging, analytics, crash reporting, app integrity, and ads. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, app activity, diagnostics, and ad interaction data according to Google policies and device settings.

06

Data Retention

We retain account data while an account is active. If account deletion is requested, the account is marked for deletion and public listings are hidden. Some information may be retained for up to 30 days for safety, fraud prevention, report review, dispute handling, and legal compliance before deletion review is completed.

Listing data, reports, and chat history may be retained where needed for investigations, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

07

Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, users may request access, correction, deletion, or objection to processing of personal data, and may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Send requests to emarketph.sup@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to confirm account ownership before processing a request.
08

Security

We use safeguards such as secure login, HTTPS/TLS transmission, Firebase security controls, and restricted database access rules. No internet service is completely risk-free, so users should keep login credentials private and use strong passwords.

09

Children's Privacy

eMarket PH is not intended for children under 13. Seller verification is limited to users who are 18 years old or older. If you believe a child created an account, contact us so we can review and remove the account where required.

10

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we may notify users in the app or by email. Continued use of eMarket PH after changes are posted means the updated policy applies.
